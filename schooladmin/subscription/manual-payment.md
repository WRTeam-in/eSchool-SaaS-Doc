# Manual Payment (Bank Transfer)

If the Super Admin has enabled it ([how the Super Admin sets it up and reviews payments](/superadmin/packages-subscription/manual-payments-cheques)), you can pay your subscription by **bank transfer or any offline method** and upload the payment receipt, instead of paying online. Your plan or add-on **starts right away**; the payment stays **Under Review** until the Super Admin checks your receipt.

---

## 1. What you can pay this way

| Paying for | Where |
|---|---|
| An unpaid **bill** | Dashboard (bill due notice) or **Subscription & Plans → Subscription** (history) |
| A **new plan** or a **plan change** (prepaid plans) | **Subscription & Plans → Plans** |
| The **renewal** (upcoming prepaid plan) | Dashboard / Subscription |
| An **add-on** (on a prepaid plan) | **Subscription & Plans → Addons** |

Where Manual Payment is available you will see a **Manual Payment** button, or, when you click a pay button, the question **"How do you want to pay?"** with **Pay online** and **Manual Payment**.

:::note
You cannot buy a new plan or change plan while you have an unpaid bill: *"Kindly settle any outstanding payments from before."* Pay the bill first.
:::

---

## 2. How to pay

1. Click **Manual Payment**.
2. The popup shows **what you are paying for**, the **Amount to pay (incl. tax)** and the **Bank details and instructions** set by the Super Admin.
3. Pay that amount to the bank account (or as the instructions say).
4. Fill in the form:

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Amount you paid** | Yes | The amount you actually transferred |
| **Transaction ID** | No | The bank / UPI reference. Strongly recommended, so the Super Admin can match your payment |
| **Payment proof** | Yes | Photo or PDF of the receipt: JPG, PNG, WEBP or PDF, up to 5 MB |
| **Note** | No | Anything the Super Admin should know |

5. Accept the terms and conditions and click **Submit**.

You will see: *"Payment submitted. Your plan is active and the payment is under review."*

---

## 3. While the payment is under review

- Your **plan or add-on is active** and you can use it normally.
- The bill shows **Under Review**.
- Your account is **not locked**, even if the bill's due date passes while it is being reviewed.
- You cannot pay the same bill, renewal or add-on online at the same time: *"A payment for this is already under review."*

---

## 4. After the review

You receive an email either way.

| Result | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| **Approved** | Nothing more to do. The bill shows as paid |
| **Rejected**: bill, new plan, plan change or renewal | Your plan **stays active**, but the bill is unpaid again. The email gives the reason and a **date to pay by**. Pay again before that date (online or with Manual Payment), otherwise your account will be locked |
| **Rejected**: add-on | The add-on **stops right away**. You can buy it again any time |
| **Closed** (paid another way) | The Super Admin recorded the payment another way, such as cash or a cheque. Nothing more is needed |

On your subscription screens a rejected bill shows the reason, so you can pay it again.

---

## 5. Paying by cheque

Cheques are recorded by the **Super Admin**, not from the school panel. Once a cheque is recorded on your bill, it shows **Cheque pending** and you get a *"Cheque received"* email. Your account is not locked while the cheque is pending. If the cheque **bounces**, you get an email with the reason and a date to pay by.

---

## 6. Common messages

| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Manual payment is not available right now.* | The Super Admin has turned Manual Payment off. Pay online or contact them |
| *A payment for this is already under review.* | You already submitted a payment for this. Wait for the review |
| *This payment is already done.* | The bill or add-on is already paid |
| *You already have an active plan.* | Use plan change or renewal instead of a new plan |
| *This plan cannot be started now.* | The plan is not a prepaid plan, is your current plan, or is not available |
| *This add-on cannot be bought now.* | Add-ons need an active prepaid plan, and the add-on must be enabled |
| *You presently have access to this functionality as part of your current subscription* | The feature is already in your plan |
