# Attendance Reports

The **Attendance Reports** module gives school administrators powerful tools to analyze and export staff attendance data across various dimensions.

![Attendance Report](/images/schooladmin/attendance-report.png)

## Available Reports

The reporting interface is divided into five tabs to help you drill down into the data you need:

1. **Date-wise:** Analyze attendance metrics grouped by date.
2. **Shift-wise:** Review attendance patterns based on assigned shifts.
3. **Status-wise:** Filter and view attendance based on the final status (Present, Absent, Half Day).
4. **Punch log:** Access the raw scan events and punch history for all staff members.
5. **Corrections:** Review the audit trail of all manual overrides and corrections made to the attendance records.
