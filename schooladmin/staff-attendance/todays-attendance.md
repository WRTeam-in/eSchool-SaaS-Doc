# Today's Attendance

The **Today's Attendance** screen provides a live floor view of your staff's attendance for the current day. 

![Today's Attendance](/images/schooladmin/today-attendance.png)

## Overview

This screen allows school administrators to monitor staff attendance in real-time. Key information displayed includes:
- **Live floor view:** See the per-staff state (on duty / off duty / open session / no punch / on leave).
- **Punch details:** First in, last out, and total punch count.
- **Working minutes:** Total working minutes accumulated so far.
- **Summary tiles:** Quick glance at the day's attendance metrics.
- **Latest scans:** A feed of the latest 15 scans.
- **Finalization status:** The finalization time, when the day was last finalized, and how many staff currently have no shift assigned.
