# Attendance Register

The **Attendance Register** allows school administrators to review finalized attendance data, perform manual corrections if necessary, and export reports.

## Daily Register View

This view displays the finalized attendance for a specific day. You can use various filters (shift, status, source, missing punch out) to find the information you need.

![Attendance Register](/images/schooladmin/attendance-register.png)

## Monthly Matrix View

The whole-month matrix provides a broad overview of staff attendance over a given month, allowing you to quickly spot trends and anomalies.

![Attendance Register Month](/images/schooladmin/attendance-register-month.png)

## Staff Day Detail

By clicking on a specific row in the register, you can open the **Staff Day Detail** view. This view provides a session-by-session breakdown showing:
- Exactly how the working minutes were counted
- The shift snapshot
- The raw punch list
- Correction history
- The manual correction form for making adjustments
