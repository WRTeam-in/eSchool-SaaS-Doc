# Academy Setup Wizard

The **Academy Setup Wizard** is a streamlined onboarding tool designed to help school administrators configure their academic structure quickly and accurately. This step-by-step guide ensures that all core components—from session years to subject mapping—are properly initialized before you begin daily operations.

## 🚀 Step-by-Step Configuration

The wizard is divided into several logical sections to ensure no detail is missed:

### 1. Welcome & Overview
The starting point where you get an overview of the key features you're about to configure, including Stream Mapping, Elective Groups, and Unified Academic Views.

### 2. Academy Year (Session)
Define your operational session:
- **Session Name**: (e.g., 2025-2026)
- **Duration**: Set the Start and End dates for the academic year.
- **Board**: Select the educational board (optional).
- **Semester System**: Enable this if your school follows a semester-based curriculum, and define individual semesters.

### 3. Mediums & Sections
Select the instructional languages (Mediums) and the operational sections (e.g., Primary, Secondary) available in your school.

### 4. School Shifts
Configure the timing shifts your school operates in (e.g., Morning Shift, Afternoon Shift). You can add multiple shifts as required.

### 5. Academic Streams
Select the educational streams offered by your institution, such as Science, Commerce, Arts, or Vocational streams.

### 6. School Grades (Classes)
Choose the classes or grades that will be active in your academy for the current session.

### 7. Master Subjects
Create a master list of all subjects taught across all grades in your school. This list will be used for mapping in the next step.

### 8. Subject & Stream Mapping
This is the most critical step where you link everything together:
- **Map Classes to Mediums**: Assign which languages are used for which grades.
- **Assign Shifts**: Define which classes operate in which shifts.
- **Stream Assignment**: For higher grades, assign specific streams.
- **Semester/Term Mapping**: If enabled, assign subjects to specific semesters.
- **Elective Groups**: Create groups for elective subjects, allowing students to choose from a set of available options.

### 9. Ready to Initialize
Review your entire configuration in the summary panel. Once verified, click **Complete Setup** to initialize your academy database. The system will process your data and redirect you to the main dashboard.

---

:::tip[Pro Tip]
You can always skip the wizard and configure these settings manually from the respective modules, but using the Wizard is recommended for a consistent and conflict-free setup.
:::
